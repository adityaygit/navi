## Now: free public beta
Waitlist / pre-launch. During the beta every feature is free, so we can gather feedback and learn usage before subscriptions. Don't introduce payments or restrict beta features.

## Planned post-beta tiers
- **Navi Free: $0 forever.** Unlimited tasks, calendars, habits and routines. Basic AI assistance with limited usage. Essential planning and organization.
- **Navi Plus: $4.99/month or $47.99/year.** Everything in Free, plus expanded AI conversations and personalized memory, intelligent scheduling and AI-assisted planning, and AI-generated daily briefings and reviews. An AI-powered productivity system.
- **Navi Ultra: $14.99/month or $143.99/year.** Everything in Plus, plus proactive, agentic assistance, background monitoring and automated workflows, advanced reasoning and AI voice conversations, connected-app intelligence and integrations, and higher AI usage allowances. A personal AI operating system that actively helps run your life.

## Positioning
- Free: Organize your life.
- Plus: Plan your life intelligently.
- Ultra: Let Navi proactively help run your life.
