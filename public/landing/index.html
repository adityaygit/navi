The same plan, your way in.

Today, in two lines each.

  • Fixed commitments are white with a lock; set-aside time is pale blue.
  • What's happening now and what's next sit at the top.
  • Free gaps are marked, with something that would fit.

Open a block to act on it.

  • Start focus, mark it done, move it, or ask Navi about it.
  • Deadlines show as a tag, and moving past one needs your OK.
  • Navi says why a block is where it is.

The week at a glance.

  • Each day as a bar, coloured by what it's for.
  • Clashes and due dates are flagged on the day they land.
  • Tap a day to open it.

When a day is too full, it says so.

  • A packed-day banner counts what's planned and any clashes.
  • "Lighten it" asks Navi to suggest what could move.
  • Suggestions come as cards. Nothing moves until you say.

Rules you can see.

Navi's plan follows a few plain rules, and it tells you when it breaks one for a reason.

Locked means fixed.

Lectures, shifts and appointments never move. Navi plans around them.

Clashes, side by side.

When two things overlap you see both at once, with a nudge to sort it out.

Check-ins at your times.

A morning plan and an evening wrap-up, by default at 6:00 and 21:00. Change them or turn them off.

Talk it through.

Hold Navi's orb and speak. It answers out loud, you can cut in at any time, and the whole conversation is saved to your chat as text.

Press and hold the orb, like in the app.

Made for real conversation.

  • Press and hold to start, so it never starts by accident. A quick tap just tells you to hold.
  • Talk over Navi and it stops to listen.
  • Mute, switch to typing, or end with one tap.
  • What you say is saved to the chat as text. The audio isn't kept.
  • Voice and typing share one history, so you can pick up either way.

Set it, then forget the phone.

Focus runs on the sky, with the time and nothing else. Holding to start and to finish early means it never begins or ends by accident.

  • Choose 25, 50 or 90 minutes, or the time the block was planned for.
  • Hold for a second to begin, then a 3·2·1.
  • Leave the screen and a small pill keeps the time.
  • Lock the phone and a "Focus done" notification arrives at the end.
  • A fixed event ten minutes away breaks through with a buzz. Nothing else does.
  • Notifications and the breakthrough buzz are being checked on real phones during early access.

Navi works for you, in the open.

An assistant that plans your life has to be easy to check. These are the rules Navi follows today, shown with real pieces of the app.

Small changes happen, with Undo.

Inside your own plan, Navi can move blocks and add tasks straight away. Every change shows as a receipt in the chat, and Undo puts it back.

Anything that leaves Navi waits for you.

A message, an email, anything sent from your account, is shown as a card first. It's marked "Leaves Navi", it says it can't be undone, and it only goes when you press and hold.

Guesses are labelled as guesses.

When Navi infers something you didn't say, it's marked "Navi noticed this" and waits for you to say whether it's right.

Memory you can read and edit.

Everything Navi remembers is written as plain sentences, with where it came from. Change it or delete it whenever you like.

Counted in the open.

Progress shows what you kept, focused on and let slip, and every number has a "How these are counted" link behind it.

Your voice: text kept, audio not.

Voice conversations are saved to your chat as text, so you can read them back. The audio itself isn't kept.

Why Navi exists

The hard part of a busy life usually isn't writing things down. It's deciding what to do next, keeping the promises you made to yourself, and not drowning in the tools that were meant to help.

Navi is built to carry that weight with you. You talk to it like a person. It turns what you say into a real plan with real tasks, routines and goals, and then it stays with you through the week: nudging, protecting your focus, and telling you honestly how it went.

It's also built to be trusted. It does the small things on its own and shows you what it did. It asks before anything consequential, and everything it remembers about you is there to read.

Privacy & control

What Navi stores, who can see it, and the things it will never do without asking you first.

What Navi keepsWhat Navi never keepsWho can see itWho processes itApprovalYour controlsSecurity

What Navi keeps

  • Your account email
  • Your plans, tasks, projects, goals and routines
  • Your conversations with Navi, including voice transcripts
  • Memories, each with where Navi learned it
  • A log of what changed and who changed it, so it can be undone

What Navi never keeps

  • Raw voice recordings. Audio is turned into text and discarded
  • Your password in readable form
  • Anything you skipped during setup

Who can see it

  • Only you. Each person's data is walled off at the database level
  • Navi is invite-only while in early access
  • Nobody at Navi reads your plans or conversations to improve the product

Who processes it

  • Text conversations are processed by Anthropic's Claude API
  • Voice conversations are processed by Google's Gemini API
  • Only what's needed to answer is sent, and it isn't used to advertise to you

Approval

Anything that leaves Navi waits for your yes. Navi works on its own inside your plan: moving flexible tasks, protecting focus time, drafting tomorrow. Anything that reaches other people, costs money, or moves something fixed is shown to you first.

Your controls

  • Quiet hours. Pick the hours when Navi stays silent. Briefings wait until they end.
  • Export everything. Download all of your data as one file whenever you like.
  • Delete your account. Permanently removes your account and everything in it.

Security

Found a security problem? Email support@mail.meetnavi.app. We'll get back to you quickly.
