Why Navi exists

The hard part of a busy life usually isn't writing things down. It's deciding what to do next, keeping the promises you made to yourself, and not drowning in the tools that were meant to help.

Navi is built to carry that weight with you. You talk to it like a person. It turns what you say into a real plan with real tasks, routines and goals, and then it stays with you through the week: nudging, protecting your focus, and telling you honestly how it went.

It's also built to be trusted. It does the small things on its own and shows you what it did. It asks before anything consequential, and everything it remembers about you is there to read.

Privacy & control

What Navi stores, who can see it, and the things it will never do without asking you first.

What Navi keepsWhat Navi never keepsWho can see itWho processes itApprovalYour controlsSecurity

What Navi keeps

  • Your account email
  • Your plans, tasks, projects, goals and routines
  • Your conversations with Navi, including voice transcripts
  • Memories, each with where Navi learned it
  • A log of what changed and who changed it, so it can be undone

What Navi never keeps

  • Raw voice recordings. Audio is turned into text and discarded
  • Your password in readable form
  • Anything you skipped during setup

Who can see it

  • Only you. Each person's data is walled off at the database level
  • Navi is invite-only while in early access
  • Nobody at Navi reads your plans or conversations to improve the product

Who processes it

  • Text conversations are processed by Anthropic's Claude API
  • Voice conversations are processed by Google's Gemini API
  • Only what's needed to answer is sent, and it isn't used to advertise to you

Approval

Anything that leaves Navi waits for your yes. Navi works on its own inside your plan: moving flexible tasks, protecting focus time, drafting tomorrow. Anything that reaches other people, costs money, or moves something fixed is shown to you first.

Your controls

  • Quiet hours. Pick the hours when Navi stays silent. Briefings wait until they end.
  • Export everything. Download all of your data as one file whenever you like.
  • Delete your account. Permanently removes your account and everything in it.
