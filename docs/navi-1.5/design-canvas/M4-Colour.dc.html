Rules

  1. The base stays near-black. Colour enters in four places only: scenes, the main button (solid, in the moment’s colour), highlighted words, and data. One gradient in the whole app: the button that starts a focus session.
  2. One palette at a time. The current moment decides it: time of day on the home pages, the space during focus, the story’s own palette in a story.
  3. Meaning colours win. Amber, mint, coral and lavender mean the same thing in every palette and are never used as decoration.
  4. Text on a coloured button is near-black; coloured text is used only at 15 px+ and weight 600+, on dark.
  5. Reduce Transparency or Reduce Motion: scenes become flat gradients, and the stone stops breathing.
  6. “Navi’s colour” in Settings (already in the app): “Follow the day” by default, or pin one palette.
