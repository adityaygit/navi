Three pages. Swipe, or tap the labels.

Left looks back, the middle is now, right looks ahead. Everything else covers the pages and closes back to where you were.

Progress ← Navi → Plan
1 · PROGRESS — LOOKING BACK

Today so far, the week planned vs done, one thing Navi noticed, and the reviews. Real numbers only, no scores.

2 · NAVI — HOME, OPENS HERE

The chat. A slim now/next line on top; Navi's first line comes from your day. Proposals change nothing until you press them; everything Navi did has Undo.

3 · PLAN — DAY (DEFAULT)

Inbox pinned on top when something waits. Timeline with fixed, flexible and routine marks; free stretches offer a fit. “Need a time” tray bottom-left, stone bottom-right.

3 · PLAN — WEEK AND LIST

Same page, other views: Week is the busy-day overview (tap a day to open it), List is every task. No sideways swipes inside pages; tap the circle to finish.

☰ · CHATS AND ACCOUNT

Only from the ☰ button, so it never fights the swipe to Progress. Your account bottom-left opens Settings, Spaces, Memory, Notes, Activity.

INBOX · SHEET FROM PLAN

What needs your OK first, then drafts with the words they came from. “You said this” vs “Navi inferred”. Nothing is on your plan until you accept it.

VOICE · COVERS EVERYTHING

Mute, Type, End. The arrow shrinks it to a talking bar so you can keep using the pages; the transcript lands in the same chat.

FOCUS · COVERS EVERYTHING

Started from a block or a task. Navi holds non-urgent things until you're done. The Lock Screen timer comes in a later board.
