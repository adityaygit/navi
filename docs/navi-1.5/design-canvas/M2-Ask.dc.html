Asking Navi without leaving Plan

No floating orb. Plan and Progress end in a resting bar that says what Navi will look at. It opens a half-height sheet with that context, grows into the full conversation, and closes back to the same screen.

1 · RESTING

The bar reads “Ask about Friday…”. Swipe or tap the stone at the top for the full chat instead.

2 · SELECT SOMETHING

Tap the essay. It opens in place, and the bar now asks about it.

3 · HALF SHEET, IN CONTEXT

“About Essay: write the intro”. Your day stays visible above. Type or talk.

4 · A PREVIEW, NOT A PROMISE

Navi shows Saturday with the essay in it. Nothing moves until you press the button.

5 · GROWN INTO THE FULL CHAT

Pull up, or tap the arrows: it is the same conversation as the Navi page, with this part marked “From Plan”.

6 · BACK WHERE YOU WERE

Close returns to Plan, updated, with Undo for a few seconds.

ON PROGRESS

Same pattern, reflective: “About this week”.
