M3 · Refined chat, a stress-tested Plan, and a Progress page with substance

Built on M2. What changed: explicit actions, one timeline scale, a visible view switch, overlaps and approvals under load, stones with a clear meaning, correctable reflections, and an evening review you talk through.

Chat

Same layout as M2. Clearer layers: Navi’s words, small grey receipts for what’s done, and one strong card when Navi needs a decision. Buttons say exactly what happens.

FRESH SESSION

Unchanged hero. The suggestion now names the action: “Add ‘Get Maya a gift’ to Saturday”. Tap the stone to talk.

FRESH · SOMETHING URGENT

Urgent beats the greeting: it shrinks to one line, and the hard deadline leads with “Start the essay now · 90 min”.

ACTIVE CONVERSATION

Kept exactly as you left it. “While you were away” marks what’s new. Anything that messages someone says so before you press it.

OFFLINE

A quiet chip, not a banner. Your message waits with “Sends when you’re back online”. Talk is dimmed, because voice needs a connection.

Plan

Day · Week · Tasks is now a visible switch. One scale for every day: 48 px an hour, short things never below a 52 px touch target, long gaps folded with a label, overlaps side by side.

NORMAL DAY

Calm as in M2. The free 30 minutes offers “Add dentist call”.

BUSY DAY

Three approvals in one amber row. A 15-minute call is still a readable block. The hard deadline is in amber on the essay itself.

BUSY · SCROLLED TO THE OVERLAP

Sam’s call sits beside the shift, both edged in amber, with “Sort it out” under them. The top bar stays readable while you scroll.

LONG GAPS FOLD

Saturday: 6½ free hours become one 56 px break, so the day still fits.

WEEK, WITH THE OVERLAP

Overlaps show as a second lane, outlined in amber.

TASKS

Unchanged list, plus a “Needs a time” group.

INBOX · 3 APPROVALS

Each approval says what changes and what it costs. Buttons name the action: “Cancel the class”, “Send to Sam”.

Progress

Stones measure what you kept of what you planned for yourself. They don’t measure hours or how full the day was. Every reflection shows its evidence and can be corrected. The evening review is a conversation.

YOUR WEEK

How the stones work, in one line. The reflection names its sources. “What Navi learned” lets you approve or reject a pattern before it changes your plans.

TAP A STONE

The evidence for that day: what you kept, what moved with notice (with your own words), and what doesn’t count.

“NOT QUITE”

Correcting a reflection opens a conversation. What you say becomes memory you can see and delete.

TONIGHT

At wrap-up time the page becomes tonight.

WRAP-UP · IN PROGRESS

One item at a time, answered with a tap or in your own words. Already-marked things show as receipts.

WRAP-UP · DONE

A short summary and one next step: see tomorrow. No score.
