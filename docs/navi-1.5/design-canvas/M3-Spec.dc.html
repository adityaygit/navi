How it behaves, not just how it looks

The rules a native build has to follow: what the stone does, how the timeline scales, gestures, motion, haptics, keyboard, safe areas, and what happens when things load slowly, go offline or get interrupted. Live Activities, haptics and the keyboard behaviour need native code whatever the app shell turns out to be.

The stone

On the chat, the stone is Navi. Tap it to talk; hold it to talk while held. At the top, the stone mark takes you to the chat, or jumps to the latest message if you’re already there. It never pretends the microphone is on.

Idle

Breathes slowly (6 s). Still when Reduce Motion is on.

Listening

The rim swells with your voice level. Only after you tap or hold.

Thinking

A slow light circles while a reply or an action is in progress.

Speaking

Soft ripples follow Navi’s voice. Tap to interrupt.

Needs you

An amber dot when an approval is waiting. Never a pop-up.

Offline

Grey. Typing still works and waits; talking is off.

One timeline scale

48 px per hour, with two exceptions that keep the day usable on a phone. Overlaps sit side by side with an amber edge.

15-minute call
Never below 52 px, so it can always be tapped and read. Shows its exact times, so height alone never misleads.
90-minute block
To scale: 48 px per hour, everywhere, every day.
3-hour shift
To scale. Long fixed things look long.
Gap under 15 minutes
Collapses to 8 px with no label.
Gap 15–45 minutes
To scale, no label, unless something fits there (“Add dentist call”).
Gap 45 minutes to 2 hours
To scale, labelled “1 hour free”.
Gap over 2 hours
Folded into a 56 px break labelled “6½ hours free”, so a quiet afternoon doesn’t push the evening off screen.

Gestures

  • Swipe left or right anywhere to change page. Nothing inside a page scrolls sideways, so there are no conflicts.
  • Every page name is also a button; VoiceOver users get “Page 2 of 3”.
  • Tap a block to open it in place. Long-press for a menu (Start focus, Move, Ask Navi, Mark done).
  • Hold the stone to talk; release to send. Tap it to start a normal voice conversation.
  • Sheets: drag the handle up to grow, down to shrink or close. A fast flick skips a height.
  • Pull down on Plan to refresh; the swipe-back edge is used only inside pushed screens.

Motion

  • Page change: 340 ms, slight overshoot; the page names travel with the finger.
  • Sheet heights: spring, no bounce past the top.
  • An accepted proposal: the block slides into the timeline with a 1.2 s green glow.
  • The stone grows into the voice screen (shared element, 300 ms).
  • Reduce Motion: every move becomes a 150 ms cross-fade; the stone stops breathing.

Haptics (few, and meaningful)

  • Success tick: a proposal accepted, a task done, focus finished.
  • Light tap: long-press menu, holding the stone to talk.
  • Selection tick: Day · Week · Tasks, choosing a day.
  • Warning: only when an approval that costs money or messages someone is confirmed.
  • None for page swipes, scrolling or sheet movement.

Keyboard

  • The composer rides on top of the keyboard; the conversation stays pinned to the latest message.
  • Typing in a half sheet grows it to full, so the reply stays in view.
  • Drag down on the conversation to put the keyboard away.
  • Drafts are kept per place (chat, each sheet context) and survive leaving the app.
  • Return sends; a newline needs Shift (hardware keyboard) or a long message.

Safe areas and size

  • Nothing interactive in the top 54 px (status bar, Dynamic Island) or the bottom 34 px (home indicator).
  • Composer and the Ask bar sit 34 px above the bottom edge; sheets extend under the home indicator.
  • Touch targets at least 44 × 44 px; body text at least 15 px; supports Dynamic Type up to XL by wrapping, never clipping.
  • Portrait only in 1.5 (to confirm).

Loading, offline, interruptions

  • Plan opens from the copy on the phone instantly, then refreshes; skeletons only on first launch. No spinners.
  • Navi “thinking” is the stone state, not a typing indicator.
  • Offline: typing always works and waits; anything that needs the internet is dimmed with the reason.
  • A phone call pauses voice and keeps the transcript; “Resume talking” continues the same conversation.
  • Leaving the app keeps the page, scroll position, open sheet and draft. Focus continues on the Lock Screen (Live Activity, native).
  • A notification opens the exact card it’s about, not just the app.

Where M3 still falls short

  • Motion is described, not shown. Opal, Structured and Tiimo earn much of their polish in motion. These boards are still stills; the transitions need a motion prototype before we can judge them.
  • The stone is a gradient, not an object. Opal’s hero gem has depth, light and material. Navi’s stone needs a proper rendered asset (or a 3D/shader pass) to carry the brand at 88 px.
  • Short blocks lose their length. With a 52 px minimum, 15, 30 and 60 minutes look alike. Exact times are printed, but Structured shows duration more clearly with its pill heights. A thin duration marker inside small blocks may help.
  • Plan has two switches stacked if we choose navigation option C. Option A avoids that; it’s the main reason I recommend it.
  • Progress is text-heavy. It has substance now, but compared with Opal’s weekly story it reads more than it shows. A swipeable weekly story on Sundays is the obvious next step.
  • Iconography is placeholder. Stroke icons are generic; a small consistent icon set would lift the whole app.
  • Light mode, Dynamic Type at the largest sizes, and Android are not designed yet.
