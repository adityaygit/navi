Pulling between pages

The page follows your finger. The one you’re leaving becomes a rounded card that shrinks and dims; the one you’re pulling in starts blurred and sharpens as it arrives. Once it’s fully through, it’s a normal page. Light haptic tick when you cross the point of no return. Reduce Motion: a plain slide, no blur or scaling.

1 · CHAT

Resting on the chat.

2 · START PULLING LEFT

The chat lifts into a card: corners round, it shrinks a little and dims. Plan’s edge appears on the right, blurred.

3 · HALFWAY

Plan sharpens and grows as it comes in. Let go before about a third of the way and it springs back.

4 · NEARLY THROUGH

The last of the blur clears; Plan’s corners square off.

5 · SETTLED

Fully through: Plan is a normal full-screen page.

THE OTHER WAY
