Making the three pages discoverable

All three treatments are switchable in the prototype (navStyle). Swiping always works, and every page name is also a button. My pick is A: the page names are always readable, and the labels move with your finger.

A · Sliding page names (recommended)

The current page’s name is bright and centred; its neighbour sits beside it, dimmer. The names slide with your swipe, so the gesture and the label move together. Two pages away is hidden.

A ON PLAN

“Navi · Plan”: you can see there is something to the left.

A ON NAVI

All three names visible from home: “Progress · Navi · Plan”. The dot says Plan has something waiting.

B · Marks that speak

M2’s three marks, with the stone as Navi. The page name appears under the marks for a moment after every change, and always during your first week.

B ON PLAN, JUST SWIPED

Calmest option, but the names are only there briefly.

B ON NAVI

After the label fades, only the marks remain.

C · Glass capsule

A small translucent switch at the top. The most explicit option, but it’s the closest to a regular tab bar and adds a second “control” next to Plan’s own Day · Week · Tasks switch.

C ON PLAN

Two segmented controls stacked: clear, but busier.

C ON NAVI

Fine on the chat; heavier than A.
