Every vibration has a reason.

Feel is for commitment, not for getting around. The less you can take something back, the heavier it feels.

1 · Moving around is silentOpening, closing, scrolling, typing and “Not now” never vibrate.
2 · Weight follows costUndoable things are light. Heavy is kept for focus starting and anything that leaves Navi.
3 · You feel the linePulling a page or a sheet ticks once when it passes the point of no return, and again if you pull back.
4 · Hold when a slip costsTime, money, other people or your account. Everything with Undo stays a tap.

How a hold feels Press: the button sinks a little and a lighter fill sweeps across, with a tick. Two more ticks at a third and two thirds. At the end: the button pops back and the commit feel fires. Let go early and the fill drains away, silently. Keyboard: hold Enter or Space. In the app, screen readers get a confirm step instead of a hold.
Rules in code One feel per action. Ticks closer than 40 ms are dropped. A Haptics switch in Settings turns all of it off and the phone’s own setting is respected. The prototype shows a small label at the top each time a feel fires (Tweaks → Show haptics).
