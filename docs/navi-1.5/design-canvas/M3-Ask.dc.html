Asking Navi from Plan: open, expand, dismiss, update

One sheet with three heights: resting bar → half → full. It always says what it’s about, and closing it returns you to exactly where you were, with the change visible and undoable.

1 · RESTING

The bar says what Navi will look at: “Ask about Friday…”.

2 · SELECT A BLOCK

Tap the essay: it opens in place, and the bar now asks about it. Long-press opens the same actions as a menu (light haptic).

3 · HALF SHEET

Tap the bar, or drag it up. The sheet rises to half height with the context in its header; the plan stays visible and scrollable above.

4 · A PREVIEW, NAMED

The sheet grows enough to show the whole proposal. The struck-out row shows what leaves; the button says “Move to Sat 9:00 AM”.

5 · FULL CHAT

Drag up, or tap the arrows: the same conversation as the Navi page, with this part marked “From Plan”. Drag down to return to half.

6 · DISMISSED, PLAN UPDATED

Swipe down or tap ×. The sheet drops, the block leaves Friday (on Saturday it arrives with a short glow), and Undo stays for 6 seconds.

ALSO · SORTING AN OVERLAP

“Sort it out” opens the same sheet about the overlap. Messaging Sam is marked, and nothing changes until Sam agrees.
