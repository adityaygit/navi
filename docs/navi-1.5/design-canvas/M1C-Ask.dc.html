Asking Navi from Plan, without leaving it

A quiet “Ask Navi” handle sits at the bottom of Plan and Progress (no floating button). It opens a sheet that already knows what you’re looking at: the day, the selected block, a task, or the review. The sheet grows into the full chat, and closing it returns you to the same screen.

1 · SELECT A BLOCK

Tap the essay. Its actions appear on it, including Ask Navi. The handle now reads “Ask Navi about this”.

2 · THE SHEET KNOWS THE CONTEXT

“About · Essay: write the intro”. Plan stays visible above. Pull the handle up, or tap the arrows, to go full.

3 · NAVI PROPOSES

Nothing changes until you press Move it. Same proposal card as in the chat.

4 · GROWN INTO THE FULL CHAT

The same conversation as the Navi page, with the Plan part marked. Close returns to Plan.

5 · BACK ON PLAN

Same screen, updated: the essay left Friday, and the sentence says where it went.
